The rule this plan is built on

Fresh first, frozen last

All the berries, peaches and avocados from the delivery are front-loaded into Week 1, because none of it survives to Week 2. The salad greens and peppers from the top-up carry into Week 2 and then stop. From Week 3 the vegetables are frozen, which is what the top-up is mostly buying: five kinds of frozen vegetable, onions, garlic, potatoes and mushrooms all keep the full 28 days.
