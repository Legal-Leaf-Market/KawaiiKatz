Why weeks 1 and 2 are thin

What the delivered order actually feeds

Counted at four grown men a dinner, roughly ten ounces of cooked protein each. Two thin crust pizzas is one man and a half, and the same arithmetic runs through the whole delivery. Fourteen dinners were asked of it and it holds seven, which is why Week 1 spends the mains properly and Week 2 runs on scraps until payday.

How $250 covers fourteen dinners

Roast Saturday, soup Sunday

Chicken leg quarters are about 89 cents a pound and dried beans are a dollar. That is what makes weeks 3 and 4 higher in protein than the delivered order despite costing less per dinner. The trick underneath it is that nothing gets cooked once: a Saturday roast is Sunday’s stock, Monday’s chili is Wednesday’s pasta bake, and the pork shoulder is both Friday’s sandwiches and Tuesday’s quesadillas.

Saturday

Roast the bag

A 10 lb bag of leg quarters, two each, potatoes underneath. Every bone goes in a freezer bag rather than the bin.

Sunday

Soup off the bones

Jake leads Sundays, which is why both soup nights are Sundays. Chicken noodle in Week 3, potato and sausage chowder in Week 4.

Monday and Wednesday

Cook once, eat twice

Monday’s chili pot is big enough that Wednesday is a beef and pasta bake made from the rest of it. The hot dish is the cheapest way to feed four men a second time.

The last night

Lentils, not crackers

September ends on lentil and sausage soup. Two pounds of dried lentils is a stockpot for three dollars, so the clean-out night is a real dinner instead of a snack board.

One shop, Thursday the 10th

The $250 list

Buy it all on payday. The first group in red is eaten straight away, because Week 2 as delivered has three nights with no meat in them at all and $47.61 fixes every one. The rest keeps: the meat goes in the freezer, the beans and rice go in the cupboard, and it feeds weeks 3 and 4. Aldi estimates rather than a receipt, and the $11.48 left over is deliberate headroom for meat prices moving.
