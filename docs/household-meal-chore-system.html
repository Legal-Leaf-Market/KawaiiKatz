What the order actually feeds

Counted at four grown men a dinner, roughly ten ounces of cooked protein each. Two thin crust pizzas is one man and a half, not one dinner, and the same arithmetic runs through the whole delivery. The order reads like a fortnight and covers a week.

DeliveredLooks likeReally isWhy
6 thin crust pizzasReads like 3 nights1 nightFour men eat four pizzas. The plan buys 2 more so Tuesday is a real pizza night and the 3 left over prop up the Friday buffet.
16 beef and bean burritosReads like 2 nights1 nightThree each is 12. The last 4 go to the Week 2 clean-out.
4 cans corned beef hashReads like 4 nights2 nightsA 15 oz can is two servings. Two cans a night, and even then it needs 6 eggs and 2 lb of potato to be dinner.
2 packs top round steakReads like 2 nights1 nightAbout 2.5 lb raw, so roughly 7 oz cooked each. Sliced thin over rice is the only honest way to serve it.
2 packs thick cut pork chopsReads like 2 nights1 nightAbout 8 chops. Two each and the pack is gone.
2 boxes corn dogs, 1 bag chicken friesReads like 3 nights1 nightFour men clear a box of corn dogs between two. This is one buffet, not three dinners.
Six mains, as deliveredAbout 16 nights7 nightsThe other seven are what the $150 buys.

The extra $150

Protein first, then everything else

The first version of this list bought sides. That was the wrong order: no quantity of frozen broccoli turns seven dinners into fourteen. Sixty dollars goes on the seven missing mains, twenty-two on the rice, potatoes, pasta and buns that actually fill four men, and the rest on vegetables, eggs and the paper goods that run out around dinner eight.
