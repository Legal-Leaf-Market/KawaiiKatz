Section five

Restock before you need it

Five places where the plan asks for more than the order holds. Counted straight off the list above, so Jake's Saturday inventory audit has somewhere to start.

  • Pizzas6 bought, 6 spent by week 3

    Two go on week one Tuesday, two on week one Saturday, one on week two Thursday and the last on week three Monday. Week four Friday's final frozen buffet has no pizza left to sweep, so either hold one back or plan that night around the chicken fries.

  • Corned beef hash4 cans, 5 hash dinners

    Week one Sunday, week two Wednesday, week three Thursday and week four Tuesday take a can each. Week four Saturday's kitchen sink hash scramble is the fifth. It already offers fried rice as the alternative, so this one costs nothing if you take that branch.

  • Eggs1 dozen, 4 weeks

    Eggs are on every week's breakfast list and on six of the twenty eight dinners. A dozen is a few days of that, not a month. This is the first thing to add to the next order.

  • Milk2 half gallons, 4 weeks

    One gallon total, against four weeks of cereal, coffee and cooking for a household this size. Expect to top up mid cycle.

  • Plastic cups50 cups, 28 dinners

    Plates at 100 and cutlery at 96 stretch across the month if they are not used at every meal. Fifty cups is the count that runs out first, and it is exactly what Elise's Saturday restock bin check is there to catch.
